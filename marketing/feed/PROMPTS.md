# ImageGen prompts

生成方式：Codex 内置 ImageGen（非 CLI fallback）。

## 获客流底图

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: vertical Douyin mini-game acquisition feed cover, portrait 3:4
Primary request: Create a polished, irresistible ad cover for this colorful gem-sorting mosaic puzzle game. The instant hook is a satisfying near-completion moment: a pair of pink tweezers is moving the final bright gem into the only obvious empty slot on a cute animal-shaped mosaic. Make the gameplay understandable in one second and create a strong urge to complete that last move.
Input images: Image 1 is the game's visual mood and background reference; Images 2 and 3 are accurate references for the small rounded faceted square gems and pixel-mosaic shapes; Image 4 is the game logo reference. Preserve the cheerful toy-like identity and gem shapes.
Scene/backdrop: clean cyan-to-lavender tabletop gradient with soft radial glow, subtle sparkles, bead trays at the lower corners, no clutter.
Subject: large central white pegboard containing a bright colorful faceted-gem animal mosaic, one single visible empty slot, tweezers holding the matching final gem just above it. Include a small strip of sorted gem tray at bottom so the sorting mechanic reads clearly.
Style/medium: premium casual mobile game key art, glossy soft 3D toy render, crisp readable silhouettes, high saturation, tactile materials.
Composition/framing: portrait 3:4, central board fills most of the image, strong close-up crop, safe margins; reserve clean space at the top for a logo/headline and at the bottom for a CTA overlay, but do not render any text or logo in the image.
Lighting/mood: bright studio lighting, joyful, playful, satisfying, high contrast around the final move.
Color palette: cyan blue, lavender, candy pink, sunny yellow, lime green, clean white.
Constraints: visually faithful to the referenced game; show only one obvious final move; no fixed level number; no rewards or coins; no UI screenshot frame; no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark.
Avoid: photorealistic hands, extra fingers, messy gem piles covering the board, dark mood, generic match-3 imagery, giant jewels, unreadable controls.
```

## 复访流底图

```text
Use case: ads-marketing
Asset type: evergreen vertical Douyin mini-game revisit feed cover for a randomized daily challenge, portrait 3:4
Primary request: Turn randomness into the visual hook. Create a polished daily mystery challenge cover for this colorful gem-sorting mosaic game. Show three overlapping premium challenge cards rising from a glowing calendar-style pedestal: the left card contains a cute animal gem mosaic, the right card contains a playful food or object gem mosaic, while the largest center card is face-down with a luminous question-mark symbol made from the same small faceted gems. The viewer should instantly understand that today's mosaic challenge is randomly selected and waiting to be revealed.
Input images: Image 1 is the game's visual mood and background reference; Images 2, 3 and 4 are accurate references for the small rounded faceted square gems and pixel-mosaic silhouettes; Image 5 is the game logo reference. Preserve the bright toy-like identity and accurate gem language.
Scene/backdrop: cyan-to-lavender radiant background, celebratory sparkles, subtle sunrise rays, a few neatly arranged gem trays at the lower corners.
Subject: three layered challenge cards, center mystery card dominant, small faceted gems sparkling around it, a tiny crown/star badge suggesting a daily special challenge, no coins.
Style/medium: premium casual mobile game key art, glossy soft 3D toy render, crisp readable silhouettes, cheerful and collectible.
Composition/framing: portrait 3:4, centered symmetric hierarchy, center mystery card fills the middle, side cards clearly different; reserve a clean upper safe area for game logo and headline and a clean lower safe area for a CTA overlay, but do not render any text or logo in the image.
Lighting/mood: bright magical morning reveal, energetic, inviting, friendly, high contrast around the mystery card.
Color palette: cyan blue, lavender, candy pink, sunny yellow, lime green, clean white, small gold accents.
Constraints: evergreen image must remain truthful for any randomly chosen level; do not show a level number, date, exact countdown, fixed board promise, reward quantity, coins, or fake UI; no text, no letters, no logo, no watermark.
Avoid: generic playing cards, photorealism, dark mood, treasure-chest gambling cues, slot machine cues, match-3 grid, giant jewels, clutter.
```
